# Dark Rifts (NeoForge 1.21.1)

Tear rifts in the sky with the **Rift Blade** and step through into the **Dark Mirror** - a perfect copy of your
overworld, drowned in eternal midnight: dead, ruined, and built from tuff, andesite, stone and deepslate.

## Build
Pushing to GitHub builds the jar automatically (`.github/workflows/build.yml`, artifact `darkrifts-jar`).
Locally: JDK 21, then `gradle build` -> `build/libs/darkrifts-1.0.0.jar`.

## Progression
1. **Ancient City** - find Echo Shards.
2. **Ritual Altar** (6 obsidian, 1 crying obsidian, 2 echo shards) - right click with an **Eye of Ender**.
3. **Rift Reaver** (300 HP, mid-game boss, comes before the Ender Dragon) - always drops a **Reaver Heart**.
4. **Mirror Blade** (one use): Reaver Heart + Iron Sword + 3 Echo Shards. Hold Right Click, look up, drag your view;
   the blade shatters when the rift is finished. Step into the rift: the Dark Mirror.
5. **Mirror Ore** grows underground in the mirror (diamond pickaxe) -> smelt -> **Mirrorite Ingot**.
6. **Mirrorite arsenal**: sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe (slightly better than diamond), **Mirror Bow** (3 ingots + 3 string).
   Sword/axe: 25% slow + weaken. Bow: +1.5 damage, pierces 2 targets.
7. **Rift Blade** (reusable, 11 abilities): Netherite Sword + 3 Mirrorite Ingots + Eye of Ender.
8. **Rift Armor** (3D: horns, spiked pauldrons, glowing core; 26 armor, toughness 4): Netherite piece + 4 Mirrorite Ingots + Eye of Ender.
   Full set: no fall damage, +4 hearts, Speed II, magic -50%, projectiles -25%, Wither immunity, Night Vision in the mirror,
   and the **Rift Ward** (8 s): a 5+ damage hit tears a rift around you, softens the blow and blasts enemies away.
9. **The Sanctum** (endgame, Dark Mirror only, **x1000 z1000**): a plateau with four 30-block towers (N/E/S/W), each with a
   **Heart Pedestal** (insert a **Reaver Heart**), and in the middle the **Egg Altar** (insert the **Dragon Egg** once all four
   hearts are in). A 16-second awakening, then the **Mirror Sovereign** (1600 HP, levitating over the dais with its own model, 3 phases) tears out of a giant rift
   and lands on the dais. Below the plateau is a crypt (spawners, ancient-city loot). The Sovereign drops the egg back, an Elytra,
   Nether Stars, Netherite and more; the altar returns and the towers reset, so the fight can be repeated.

Rifts you draw collapse after 5 minutes (`riftLifetimeSeconds`). Sneak + Right Click closes a rift with the Rift Blade.

## The Dark Mirror
* Same seed, same noise settings, same biome layout as the overworld -> identical terrain, caves, ores, tree and structure
  positions. Eternal midnight, no ambient light.
* While a chunk generates, the mod rewrites it: grass/dirt/sand/terracotta/snow -> tuff, andesite, stone, cobbled deepslate,
  deepslate, smooth basalt, gravel; all plants and leaves die; tree trunks turn into black (dark oak) dead trunks.
* Man-made blocks (villages, ...) are ruined: doors, glass, torches, ladders and every container/bed/sign vanish,
  ~45% of walls/planks/stairs crumble (some become cobwebs, cracked or mossy).
* **Your builds:** when a brand-new mirror chunk is created and the same overworld chunk is loaded, the man-made blocks you
  built there are copied in - ruined the same way, no containers, no loot. (One-time snapshot per mirror chunk.)

## Commands (op level 2)
`/darkrifts open` - permanent rift in front of you  |  `/darkrifts visit` - enter the mirror  |  `/darkrifts home`
`/darkrifts sanctum` - build the Sanctum at x1000 z1000 now (it also builds itself when a player comes within 96 blocks) and go there

## Config (`darkrifts-common.toml`)
`riftLifetimeSeconds`, `strokeRadius`, `maxRiftBlocks`.

## Visual effects
Every rift now has the ritual treatment: a thunder-and-lightning **birth show** (rune circle, soul-fire pillars, spirals of void,
ground cracks, screen flash + shake), a living tear (sparks, energy pulses and arcs along the line), a rune circle with a pillar of
light under each standing rift, an unstable flicker in the last 5 seconds and an implosion when it collapses, plus flashes and
bursts when you jump through. Code: `world/RiftFx`, `client/RiftAura`, `client/FxDraw`.

## Notes
* Armor: `tools/armor_gen.py` generates the worn textures, icons AND `client/RiftArmorParts.java` from one cube table.
* Sanctum blocks/skins: `tools/sanctum_assets.py`.
* Resources/data are generated by `python3 tools/generate_assets.py` (needs Pillow).
* Tweak the look of the dark world in `world/MirrorPalette.java` (block lists, ruin chances).
