# Dark Rifts (NeoForge 1.21.1)

Tear rifts in the sky with the **Rift Blade** and step through into the **Dark Mirror** - a perfect copy of your
overworld, drowned in eternal midnight: dead, ruined, and built from tuff, andesite, stone and deepslate.

## Build
Pushing to GitHub builds the jar automatically (`.github/workflows/build.yml`, artifact `darkrifts-jar`).
Locally: JDK 21, then `gradle build` -> `build/libs/darkrifts-1.0.0.jar`.

## How to play
* **Rift Blade** recipe: 3 Echo Shards + Netherite Sword + Eye of Ender (`" E " / "ESE" / " P "`), also in the creative tab.
* **Hold Right Click**, look at the sky and **drag your view**: the rift tears open live along your crosshair
  (lightning, portal particles, rising crackle); release to finish it with a shockwave.
  Rifts you draw collapse after 5 minutes (config `riftLifetimeSeconds`, 0 = permanent).
* **Sneak + Right Click** on a rift with the blade closes the whole rift (return rifts in the mirror stay).
* Natural rifts (ragged vertical tears) generate in the overworld; walking into one takes you to the mirror.
* In the mirror, a permanent return rift appears next to where you arrive. "Home" is the overworld at the **same X/Z**.

## The Dark Mirror
* Same seed, same noise settings, same biome layout as the overworld -> identical terrain, caves, ores, tree and structure
  positions. Eternal midnight, no ambient light.
* While a chunk generates, the mod rewrites it: grass/dirt/sand/terracotta/snow -> tuff, andesite, stone, cobbled deepslate,
  deepslate, smooth basalt, gravel; all plants and leaves die; tree trunks turn into black (dark oak) dead trunks.
* Man-made blocks (villages, ...) are ruined: doors, glass, torches, ladders and every container/bed/sign vanish,
  ~45% of walls/planks/stairs crumble (some become cobwebs, cracked or mossy).
* **Your builds:** when a brand-new mirror chunk is created and the same overworld chunk is loaded, the man-made blocks you
  built there are copied in - ruined the same way, no containers, no loot. (One-time snapshot per mirror chunk.)

## Commands (op level 2)
`/darkrifts open` - permanent rift in front of you  |  `/darkrifts visit` - enter the mirror  |  `/darkrifts home`

## Config (`darkrifts-common.toml`)
`riftLifetimeSeconds`, `strokeRadius`, `maxRiftBlocks`.

## Notes
* Resources/data are generated by `python3 tools/generate_assets.py` (needs Pillow).
* Tweak the look of the dark world in `world/MirrorPalette.java` (block lists, ruin chances).
